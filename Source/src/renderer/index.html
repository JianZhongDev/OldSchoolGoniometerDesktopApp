Goniometer Overlay v1.0.0

An always-on-top protractor with two hinged ruler arms that floats over any app — for measuring and referencing angles and distances right on your screen.

Type or paste a value (or use a real ruler to calibrate). Auto-tracks display scaling. Out-of-range entries are clamped; invalid entries are ignored.
